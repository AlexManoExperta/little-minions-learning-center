About us

A trusted place to learn and grow

For 8 years, Little Minions Learning Center has been caring for Albuquerque families. We are fully licensed and registered with the State of New Mexico — an established, legitimate business that parents can count on.

Every day is built around what children need most: a safe and loving environment, nutritious meals, and plenty of chances to play, explore and learn. With small groups and both day and evening shifts, we're here for working families when they need us.
